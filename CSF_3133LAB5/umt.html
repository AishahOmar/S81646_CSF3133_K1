About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a premier public university located in Kuala Nerus District, Terengganu, Malaysia, with an additional campus in Bukit Kor, Marang. Led by Vice-Chancellor Professor Dr. Suriyani Muhamad, UMT is ranked No. 1 in Malaysia for Marine and Freshwater Biology and operates nine academic responsibility centres alongside four specialized research institutes.

UMT began as Universiti Pertanian Malaysia's Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science in Mengabang Telipot, Kuala Terengganu, providing hands-on practical and research facilities for fisheries and marine science students. In June 1996, the Faculty of Fisheries and Marine Science of Universiti Pertanian Malaysia (UPM) in Serdang was transferred to Kuala Terengganu, establishing Universiti Pertanian Malaysia Terengganu (UPMT). Following its transition into Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM), the institution was officially granted full university status and renamed Universiti Malaysia Terengganu on 1 February 2007.

Current Academic Faculties (2026/2027 Session)

Following UMT's academic restructuring, the former Faculty of Fisheries and Food Science (FPSM) was separated into two distinct faculties—FSPA and FSMA—bringing the university to seven specialized faculties supported by the Centre for Foundation & Continuing Education (PPAL) and the STEM Foundation Centre:

Faculty of Fisheries and Aquaculture Sciences FSPA Fisheries Science, Aquaculture, and Aquatic Resource Management
Faculty of Food Sciences and Agrotechnology FSMA Food Technology, Food Service & Nutrition, Crop Science, and Agro Processing
Faculty of Science and Marine Environment FSSM Marine Science, Marine Biology, Oceanography, and Environmental Science
Faculty of Ocean Engineering Technology FTKK Maritime Technology, Naval Architecture, Electronics, and Renewable Energy
Faculty of Maritime Studies FPM Maritime Management, Maritime Operations, Nautical Science, and Logistics
Faculty of Business, Economics and Social Development FPEPS Economics, Accounting, Finance, Policy, and Social Sciences
Faculty of Computer Science and Mathematics FSKM Software Engineering, Maritime Informatics, Mobile Computing, Data Analytics, and Financial Mathematics
